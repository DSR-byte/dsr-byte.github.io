FEATURED PROJECT / 01

Vision-based robotic manipulation

Motion planning and camera calibration for a four-degree-of-freedom OpenManipulator-X arm.

  • Planned task-space poses, checked inverse-kinematics reachability, and tested pick-and-place motion on hardware.
  • Compared cubic joint-space and quintic task-space trajectories using measured path and speed data.
  • Implemented Jacobian pseudoinverse waypoint tracking with a measured 59 Hz loop and 48.2 mm/s realized speed for a 50 mm/s command.
  • Calibrated a RealSense D435 to the robot frame using AprilTags and coordinate transforms; measured 2.0 mm mean registration error.
